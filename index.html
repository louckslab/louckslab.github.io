From genetic variation to
better pain management

We study how genetic variation shapes pain treatment response. By integrating pharmacology, whole genome analysis, C. elegans functional validation, and deep patient partnership, we work toward personalized pain management that is safer and more effective for every child.

What We Study

Our approach to the future of personalized pain management

Our work follows a Discovery → Validation → Implementation pipeline — from identifying genetic predictors of drug response and functionally validating them in C. elegans, to translating findings into clinical policy through patient partnership.

Discovery

Using cohort genomic studies, our team maps the DNA variations that cause children to have adverse reactions to opioid medications. We focus on identifying risks for common side effects — including pruritus, nausea, and allergic reactions — before treatment begins.

Validation

We validate our discoveries by replicating results in additional patient populations and performing functional testing in C. elegans to establish causal evidence.

Implementation

Through our involvement in the Canadian Pharmacogenomics Network for Drug Safety (CPNDS), we bring genetic findings to the clinic — shaping clinician guidelines and regulatory policy for safer, personalized pain management.
